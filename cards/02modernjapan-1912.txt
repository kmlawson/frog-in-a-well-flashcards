Shinto Buddhist Separation edict	1868
Meiji Charter Oath promulgated	1868, Apr
Boshin War, last Tokugawa resistance	1868-9
Founding of the short-lived national deliberative assembly (Kōgisho)	1868
Peak of anti-Buddhist destruction and violence	early 1870s
Abolition of the domains, prefectures established following year	1871
Liberation of outcastes	1871
Iwakura Mission	 1871-3
First train line, connecting Tokyo and Yokohama completed	1872
Compulsory education law	1872
Prostitution emancipation act	1872
Debate by oligarchy over 'punishing Korea'	1873
New Meiji tax system established	1873
Conscription established	1873
The Meirokusha society is founded	1873
Taiwan punitive expedition	1874
"Memorial on the Establishment of a Representative Assembly" (Tosa memorial)	1874
Publication ordinance and press ordinances passed	 1875
Treaty of Saint Petersburg between Russia and Japan	1875
Kanghwa Treaty	1876
Satsuma Rebellion	1877
Provincial prefectural assemblies established	1878
Ōkubo Toshimichi assassinated	1878
The Ryūkyū islands become Okinawa prefecture	1879
Severe deflationary period (Matsukata deflation)	early 1880s
(The old) Penal Code enacted based on draft by Emile Gustave Boissonade	1880
Peak year of the popular rights movement, founding of the Liberal Party (Jiyūtō)	1881
Emperor announces the coming of a constitution	1881
Formation of the Progressive Party (Kaishintō) under Ōkuma Shigenobu	1882
The Imperial Rescript to Soldiers and Sailors	1882
Western-style ballroom Rokumeikan completed, symbol for Westernization	1883
Kapsin Coup in Korea with Japanese support	1884
Chichibu Uprising	1884
Fukuzawa's essay "On Leaving Asia"	1885

The *Normanton* Incident	1886
Meiji Constitution establishes Japan as a constitutional monarchy	1889, Feb

Imperial Rescript on Education	1890
Law on Assemby and Political Societies passed	1890

Tonghak Rebellion in Korea	1894

Anglo-Japanese Treaty (Mutsu treaty) ending extraterritoriality goes into effect	1899

Public Order Police Law severely impacts labor movement	 1900

First electric tramline opened from Shinagawa to Shinbashi	1903

Korea made into a protectorate of Japan	1905

Hibiya Riots	1905, Sep
Karafuto (South Sakhalin) becomes Japanese colony	1905
Large scale nationalization of intercity railways	1906
A much revised Penal Code enacted	1907
Itō Hirobumi assassinated by Korean independence activist, An Chunggŭn	1909
Korea is Annexed by Japan	1910, Aug
